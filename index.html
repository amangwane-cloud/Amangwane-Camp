About the Camp

A proper bush camp.
No frills, all charm.

Amangwane Tented Camp sits right on the edge of the iSimangaliso Wetland Park — just 100 metres from the Kosi Bay Mouth reserve gate. It's real, rustic and relaxed. Think sandy paths, big shady Waterberry trees, reed walls, and an outdoor lifestyle that gets you closer to nature than any hotel ever could.

Every tent has its own en-suite bathroom with hot water. Your linen and towels are sorted. The communal kitchen and braai area are set up for proper camp cooking. This is the Kosi Bay experience without the big price tag.

🌿 Shaded under big trees 🏖️ 2km from the beach 🚿 Private en-suite bathrooms 🍳 Full communal kitchen 🔥 Braai & boma 🇲🇿 5km to Mozambique 🐦 450+ bird species ♻️ Sustainable tourism
